ABOUT THE PHOTOGRAPHER

I photograph what makes me pause.

Photography started as a hobby, but somewhere between everyday scenes and unfamiliar places, I began seeing stories in light, colour and small details.

This portfolio is a selection of those moments — from familiar landscapes in Tamil Nadu to the ocean, wildlife and slower rhythms of the Maldives.
